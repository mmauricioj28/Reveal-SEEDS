Crash counts refer to retained crash records. Inspection counts refer to inspection records. Event frequency describes the extract and does not measure risk per trip or mile.

A matched record has a usable USDOT number found in the Census extract. An unmatched record has a usable number absent from that extract. An unidentified record lacks a usable number.

Crash geography uses the reported crash location. Inspection geography uses the reporting jurisdiction. The carrier views use Census attributes from the snapshot, including for events in earlier years.
